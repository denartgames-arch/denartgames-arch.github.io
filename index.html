Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Denart Games ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile applications ("Apps").

This Privacy Policy applies to Denart Games mobile applications that link to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Our Apps do not require you to create an account and do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other information that directly identifies you.

However, information may be collected automatically when you use our Apps by the Apps themselves and by third-party services integrated into them.

Technical and Usage Information

Depending on the App and the services it uses, information may include:

  • Device type, model, and manufacturer;
  • Operating system and version;
  • App version;
  • Language and general device settings;
  • IP address;
  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers;
  • Information about interactions with the App;
  • Advertising and ad interaction information;
  • App performance, diagnostic, and crash information;
  • General usage and analytics information;
  • Information related to in-app purchases and purchase verification.

2. Firebase Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC, to understand how users interact with our Apps and to improve their functionality and performance.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as device information, IP address, app usage events, diagnostic information, and identifiers. The exact information collected may depend on the App configuration and the features you use.

We use analytics information to:

  • Understand how users use our Apps;
  • Improve gameplay, features, and user experience;
  • Monitor App performance;
  • Identify crashes and technical problems;
  • Analyze general usage trends.

3. Advertising - Google AdMob

Our Apps use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements.

AdMob and related Google services may collect or process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, device information, information about advertisements displayed or interacted with, and diagnostic or performance information.

Depending on your location, device settings, applicable law, and available consent choices, this information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising, as well as to detect fraud and abuse.

Advertising identifiers can generally be reset or restricted through the privacy settings of your device.

More information about Google's handling of data for advertising can be found in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, advertising and analytics services may request your consent before certain data is processed for personalized advertising or other purposes.

Depending on the App and your location, you may be presented with privacy or consent choices when using the App. You may also be able to change certain advertising and privacy settings through your device or Google account.

5. In-App Purchases

Our Apps may offer optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through the applicable app store, including Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment card number, bank account information, or other sensitive payment credentials.

We may receive limited information related to a purchase, such as transaction information, purchase status, product information, or information needed to verify and restore purchases.

Payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider according to its own privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Services

Our Apps use third-party services that may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase - analytics and usage measurement;
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) - advertising and advertising measurement;
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing - platform services and in-app purchases.

Third-party services may process information on their own systems and according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed through our Apps may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the Apps;
  • Improve features and user experience;
  • Analyze App usage;
  • Monitor and improve performance;
  • Detect crashes, bugs, fraud, and technical problems;
  • Display, personalize, and measure advertisements where permitted;
  • Process and verify in-app purchases;
  • Restore purchased content;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because our Apps do not require user accounts, we generally do not maintain personal account profiles containing your name, email address, or similar direct identifiers.

Information collected by third-party services such as Firebase Analytics, AdMob, and Google Play may be retained according to their respective policies and retention practices.

If you have questions about information processed by our Apps or would like to contact us regarding your privacy, please use the contact information provided below.

Our Apps do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or other direct personal information from children.

Some Apps may be accessible to children. Where an App is intended for children or families, we configure its data practices and advertising features in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

Information transmitted to third-party services is protected using security measures provided by those services, including encryption where applicable.
